# Implementation Plan - Business Contacts & Unified Contacts Model

This plan introduces a new **Business Contacts (Global Contacts)** module, a **Contact Roles Master** screen, and refactors the existing **Customer Workers** to **Customer Contacts** for model unification. It also extends the active invoice page to support referencing either a Customer + Worker or a Global Business Contact.

## Open Questions

There are no major open questions, but we will assume:
- Standard cascading deletes: Deleting a Business Contact will clear mappings in `business_contact_role_map` automatically via SQLite foreign keys.
- Deleting a Role is prohibited if it is currently mapped to any Business Contact. We will check this condition before allowing deletion in the Roles Master view.

- Re-create the index `idx_customer_contacts_customer` on `customer_contacts(customer_id)`.

- Create table `contact_roles` master table containing role name, order, activity flag, and timestamps.
