# Implementation Plan - Unified Customer & Business Contact Search

Simplify the Invoice Details form by removing the manual toggle ("Invoice For: Customer Account / Business Contact") and combining customer and business contact searches under a single, unified search input field.

## Proposed Changes

### Billing Component

#### [MODIFY] [invoice-form.ts](file:///Users/smitpatel/Desktop/Personal%20Projects/voice-to-text/src/app/features/billing/components/invoice-form/invoice-form.ts)
- Replace separate search signals (`customerSearchQuery`, `contactSearchQuery`, `filteredCustomers`, `filteredContacts`, `showCustomerDropdown`, `showContactDropdown`) with unified signals:
  - `searchQuery` (signal<string>)
  - `showDropdown` (signal<boolean>)
  - `filteredResults` (signal<any[]>)
- Consolidate the two separate autocomplete search effects into a single effect that queries both `CustomerService.searchCustomer` and `BusinessContactService.searchContacts`.
- Map results from both sources into a unified structure, tagged with `type: 'customer'` or `type: 'business_contact'` and having a `uniqueId`.
- In `ngOnInit()`, subscribe to `customerName` changes and set the unified `searchQuery` signal. If `currentInvoice.customerId` is initialized, fetch initial customer contacts.
- In `initializeForm()`, determine the initial `billingType` and `customerMobile` validator settings based on `currentInvoice.contactId`.
- Create a `selectResult(item)` helper that routes to the appropriate selection method (`selectCustomer` or `selectBusinessContact`) and sets the validators accordingly.
- Update `onCustomerSearchInput`, `onCustomerFocus`, and `onCustomerBlur` to use the unified dropdown state.

#### [MODIFY] [invoice-form.html](file:///Users/smitpatel/Desktop/Personal%20Projects/voice-to-text/src/app/features/billing/components/invoice-form/invoice-form.html)
- Remove the manual "Invoice For" radio button toggle block.
- Place the "Customer Name" input field as a common field at the top of the details grid.
- Bind the input to `onCustomerSearchInput`, `onCustomerFocus`, and `onCustomerBlur`.
- Render a unified dropdown displaying search matches from both Customer Accounts and Business Contacts, using badges to distinguish them.
- When an item is selected from the dropdown, invoke the unified `selectResult(item)` handler.
- Conditionally render the remaining form fields based on whether `billingType()` is `'customer'` or `'business_contact'`.

## Verification Plan

### Manual Verification
- Run `npm run start` or verify the running `ng s` process.
- Open the Invoice page.
- Verify that the "Invoice For" toggle buttons are gone.
- Type in the "Customer Name" field (e.g. "John" or "Acme").
- Verify that the dropdown displays matching customer accounts and business contacts with respective badges.
- Select a Customer Account and verify that customer-specific fields (Company Name, Mobile, Purchased By selector) are shown and populated.
- Reset the form, type again, and select a Business Contact. Verify that contact-specific fields (Mobile optional, Contact Profession Roles badges) are shown and populated.
